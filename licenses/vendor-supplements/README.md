# Supplemental upstream license files

These are verbatim upstream license/attribution files omitted from the published
crate archives in the 0.0.1 lockfile. They retain their upstream terms and
copyright notices; Jury does not relicense them. `sources.json` records exact
URLs, revisions and SHA-256 digests consumed by the native package builder.

Revisions come from each crate's `.cargo_vcs_info.json`. The Android support
archive has no VCS record; its supplement comes from the exact upstream
revision of the locked consuming `rustls-platform-verifier` 0.7.0 crate, whose
license applies to its Android component. The supplement is not a claim that
that revision produced the Android archive. `r-efi` carries its MIT terms and
attributions in `AUTHORS`, so that file is retained intact. These supplemental
files can be removed when the corresponding locked packages carry complete
license material themselves or leave the distribution.

The `calcifer-macos-acl` 0.1.0 supplement retains the exact crate revision's
workspace MIT license, omitted from the packaged member crate.
